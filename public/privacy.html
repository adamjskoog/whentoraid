Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

WhenToRaid is a free, hobby tool for planning World of Warcraft raid nights. This page explains what it stores, why, and how to have it removed.

Using WhenToRaid without signing in

A guild created without signing in is saved only in your browser's local storage. It is never sent to WhenToRaid's servers. Clearing your browser data, or using Start over in Guild settings, removes it.

Signing in with Discord

Online guilds use Discord sign-in. When you sign in, Discord shares your user ID, username, display name, avatar and email address. These are kept with your sign-in account. WhenToRaid uses your Discord user ID to link you to your place in a guild and suggests your display name as your player name. It does not use your email address, and it never receives your password, messages, server list or friends.

What an online guild stores

  • The guild's name, time zone, raid schedule and settings, and its Discord server ID if an officer adds it.
  • Each member's player name, Discord user ID, officer status and characters (name, realm, class, spec and role).
  • Weekly availability, check-ins and away days.
  • Rosters, attendance, and players' raid responses (confirmed or can't attend, with an optional note).
  • Discord connection details set by officers: a webhook URL or bot channel ID, the IDs of messages WhenToRaid posted, and reminder settings.
  • Private calendar feed links, if a player creates one.

Members of a guild can see that guild's data. Some settings, such as webhook URLs, are visible only to officers.

The Discord bot

If a guild connects the WhenToRaid bot, it posts rosters and reminders to the channel officers choose and can mention rostered players. When you press a button on one of its posts, WhenToRaid receives your Discord user ID and the server, channel and message, and records your answer on the roster. It keeps the button press's ID to ignore repeats. The bot does not read other messages in your server.

Services WhenToRaid relies on

  • Supabase hosts sign-in accounts, the database and server functions for online guilds (United States).
  • GitHub Pages serves the website.
  • Discord provides sign-in and delivers posts to your server.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts the app uses.

These services may log technical data such as your IP address when your browser contacts them, under their own privacy policies. WhenToRaid has no ads, analytics or tracking, and never sells or shares your data.

Keeping and removing your data

Guild data stays until an officer changes or removes it. When an officer removes a member, that member's characters, availability and responses are deleted with them. To have your account or your guild's data removed, ask a guild officer, or open a request on GitHub. Don't include personal details in a public request; say how to reach you and we'll follow up.

Children

WhenToRaid is not meant for children under 13, and Discord requires its users to be at least 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
